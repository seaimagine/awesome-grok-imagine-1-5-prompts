# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve this Grok Imagine 1.5 prompt collection.

## What belongs here

A strong contribution is:

- original writing created by you for this repository;
- designed for Grok Imagine 1.5 rather than copied from another model's gallery;
- specific about mode, duration, aspect ratio, subject continuity, camera, physical motion, and audio;
- practical enough that another creator can customize it;
- free from third-party creator names, handles, source-post links, or promotional copy;
- safe to publish and respectful of privacy, likeness, trademark, and copyright.

Do not submit scraped prompts, lightly paraphrased third-party examples, copyrighted characters, celebrity likenesses, misleading endorsements, non-consensual intimate content, or media you do not have permission to distribute.

## Prompt format

````markdown
## Descriptive title

**Mode:** Text-to-video · **Duration:** 10s · **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Audio:** ambience + dialogue

```text
[Copy-ready prompt with subject lock, timeline, camera, physics, audio, continuity, and avoid constraints.]
```
````

Keep the main title useful for search. Avoid hype-only titles such as “Insane Prompt #27.”

## Preview media

Preview images and videos must be newly created for the contribution and licensed for repository use. Use fictional, unbranded subjects by default. Remove private metadata and do not include visible signatures, usernames, third-party watermarks, or platform UI.

Recommended image format:

- PNG or WebP;
- 16:9 for category previews unless the prompt specifically requires another ratio;
- meaningful lowercase filename with hyphens;
- descriptive alt text in Markdown.

## Pull request checklist

- [ ] The prompt is original and not a lightly rewritten third-party prompt.
- [ ] Mode, duration, ratio, camera, physics, audio, continuity, and avoid constraints are internally consistent.
- [ ] Dialogue fits the allotted time and is quoted exactly.
- [ ] Real people, brands, private media, and copyrighted characters are absent or fully authorized.
- [ ] Links are valid and use HTTPS.
- [ ] Markdown renders correctly.
- [ ] The prompt count in `README.md` and `prompts/README.md` is updated if needed.

By contributing, you agree that your submitted original material may be distributed under the repository's [MIT License](LICENSE).
