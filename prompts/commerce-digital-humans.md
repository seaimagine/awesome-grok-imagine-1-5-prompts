# Commerce, Livestream, and Digital Human Prompts for Grok Imagine 1.5

![Original creator commerce preview](../assets/commerce-digital-human.png)

These short-form prompts prioritize truthful product geometry, natural presenters, concise spoken copy, and demonstrations that fit inside 15 seconds.

## 1. Modular Desk Lamp — livestream feature demo

**Mode:** Reference-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 12s · **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 · **Audio:** presenter dialogue + product foley

```text
Use the product reference to preserve the exact unbranded modular desk lamp: matte ivory finish, circular base, two articulated arms, three visible hinge joints, thin oval light head, one touch button, and no cable in frame. Use the presenter reference only for the same fictional adult face, short dark hair, sage long-sleeve shirt, and natural skin texture.

Create a 12-second vertical creator-livestream demonstration in a compact home studio.

0–3s — medium shot; presenter looks into the main camera, places one hand on the lamp base, and says, "Three joints, so the light goes where the work is." Product remains fully visible.
3–7s — close product shot; presenter adjusts the lower hinge, then the upper hinge, one at a time. The lamp head moves over a blank notebook while the base stays flat and the joints rotate only on their real axes.
7–10s — fingertip taps the one button; light changes from neutral white to warm white in one smooth step, with shadows and camera exposure adjusting naturally.
10–12s — return to medium shot. Presenter points at the evenly lit notebook and says, "That's the whole desk, not my whole room." Hold the product for half a second.

CAMERA: Authentic high-end phone livestream look with two clean cuts; no crane, orbit, or fake UI overlay.

AUDIO: Quiet creator room, hinge friction, button click, exact dialogue with friendly natural lip sync. No background music.

AVOID: new joints, bending arms, floating base, invented controls, changing lamp color, sales price, captions, logos, watermark.
```

## 2. Reversible Jacket — concise on-body clothing showcase

**Mode:** Image-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 10s · **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 · **Audio:** fabric + voiceover

```text
Animate the supplied full-body fashion reference. Preserve the fictional adult model's identity, body proportions, hairstyle, black trousers, shoes, and the exact reversible jacket: rust-orange outer side, midnight-blue inner side, silver zipper, two pockets, fixed seam map, and no logo.

0–3s — model faces three-quarter left in a neutral studio, closes the silver zipper once, then turns toward camera; medium shot shows fit through shoulders and waist.
3–6s — close detail shot follows one hand opening the left pocket and placing a plain card inside; seam and fabric texture remain accurate.
6–8s — use a fast full-frame coat-hem wipe as the model turns. After the wipe, the same jacket is correctly reversed to midnight blue, with rust-orange lining visible only at cuffs and collar.
8–10s — full-body shot; model takes one natural step, stops, and opens both hands to show the final silhouette.

VOICEOVER: "Two finishes, one familiar fit, and pockets that actually hold something."

PHYSICS: Jacket drape has medium weight, zipper teeth remain aligned, card enters a real pocket, fabric settles after the turn.

AVOID: changing model, new pockets, different zipper, instant body morph, exposed or unsafe styling, brand label, on-screen text, watermark.
```

## 3. Two-Language Travel Adapter Explainer

**Mode:** Reference-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 13s · **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Audio:** English + Spanish dialogue

```text
Use the reference image to lock one fictional adult presenter and one unbranded universal travel adapter with two slide controls, one front socket, and two USB-C ports. Preserve face, navy overshirt, adapter geometry, colors, port count, and tabletop layout.

0–4s — presenter holds the adapter beside the face, looks at camera, and says in English, "Slide the country pin out before you plug in." Their thumb moves only the left control; the correct metal pins extend mechanically.
4–8s — macro top-down view. The adapter is placed on the table; a plain USB-C cable connects to the left USB-C port. Presenter continues in Spanish, "Y conecta el cable solo después de fijarlo." Voice remains the same person with natural language-specific pronunciation.
8–13s — medium shot. Presenter points to the extended pins and connected cable, then says in English, "Check the voltage on your device, too." End on the unpowered tabletop demonstration.

CAMERA: Clear tutorial coverage, one speaker, two cuts, no fast social-media zooms.

AUDIO: Exact bilingual dialogue, slide click, cable insertion, quiet room tone. No music and no device power sounds.

SAFETY: Demonstrate only on the table; do not insert the adapter into a wall outlet. Do not make universal safety or compatibility claims.

AVOID: extra ports, changing pin type, unreadable mouth movement, subtitles, flags, UI graphics, logos, watermark.
```

## 4. Compact Garment Steamer — problem/solution creator ad

**Mode:** Image-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 12s · **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 · **Audio:** creator dialogue + steam

```text
Animate the reference product and creator into a 12-second vertical home demonstration. Preserve the exact unbranded steamer body, translucent water tank, cord, single trigger, gray fabric head, creator identity, white shirt, and the same wrinkled linen overshirt hanging safely on a wooden hanger.

0–3s — close-up of one naturally wrinkled sleeve. Creator enters frame holding the cold steamer and says, "This is the sleeve I always give up on." No steam yet.
3–5s — creator presses the trigger; after a realistic short delay, a controlled stream of vapor begins. One indicator light turns from amber to green.
5–9s — side view shows one slow downward pass with the steamer head near, but not crushing, the hanging fabric. Wrinkles relax only along the completed path; untreated fabric remains wrinkled.
9–12s — creator holds treated and untreated sections side by side and says, "One pass. That's enough for me." Steam stops when the trigger is released.

PHYSICS: Vapor rises and disperses, tank water level changes imperceptibly, cord follows gravity, fabric remains on the hanger.

AUDIO: Trigger click, gentle boiler and steam, exact conversational dialogue. No music.

AVOID: steamer touching skin, instant full-garment transformation, redesigned tank, new buttons, exaggerated claim, price text, logo, captions, watermark.
```

## 5. Noise-Reducing Earbuds — honest commute demonstration

**Mode:** Reference-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 11s · **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 · **Audio:** controlled ambience comparison

```text
Use Reference Image 1 for the exact fictional adult commuter and Reference Image 2 for the exact unbranded earbuds and charging case. Preserve the person's face, short curly hair, olive jacket, earbud shape, case hinge, color, and left/right orientation.

0–3s — vertical handheld shot on a stationary train platform. The commuter holds the open case toward camera and says, "I use these when the platform gets loud." A train is visible approaching slowly in the background at a safe distance.
3–7s — profile close-up. The commuter removes one right earbud, inserts it correctly, then taps it once. Ambient train and crowd sound lowers smoothly but remains faint; do not imply total silence.
7–11s — medium shot. Commuter relaxes their shoulders, closes the case, and says, "Quieter, not disconnected." The arriving train stops behind the safety line.

CAMERA: Believable creator phone video, three simple shots, consistent platform side and safe framing.

AUDIO: Platform ambience, train brakes, case click, one tap, exact dialogue. The sound comparison is gradual and honest; announcements remain faintly audible. No music.

AVOID: unsafe track position, invisible earbud, ear anatomy distortion, duplicated case, absolute-noise-cancellation claim, brand, captions, watermark.
```
