## 1. Sparkling Water Maker — authentic morning UGC

**Mode:** Reference-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 10s · **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 · **Audio:** creator dialogue + product foley

```text
Create a 10-second vertical UGC ad using the supplied image only to lock the unbranded sparkling-water maker. Preserve its exact silhouette, white finish, button position, bottle shape, nozzle, proportions, and counter placement.

CREATOR: A fictional adult home creator in a plain sage T-shirt. Natural, unscripted energy; no beauty filter.

0–2s — close handheld shot as the creator locks a water bottle into the machine. 2–5s — thumb presses the top button; tight product shot shows dense bubbles forming in the water while the machine stays rigid on the counter. 5–8s — creator releases the button, removes the bottle, and pours into a glass with one lemon slice. 8–10s — casual sip and direct-to-camera line: "Okay, that is actually crisp." End with the machine clearly visible behind the glass.

CAMERA: Premium smartphone look, subtle realistic hand movement, quick motivated cuts, no cinematic crane shots.

PHYSICS: Carbonation bubbles rise naturally; bottle does not deform; pour has continuous volume; condensation remains consistent.

AUDIO: Button click, short carbonation hiss, fizz, pour, quiet kitchen room tone. Dialogue is conversational and lip-synced. No music.

AVOID: brand labels, invented buttons, changing product geometry, excessive foam, extra fingers, fake influencer gestures, captions, text, watermark.
```

## 2. Commuter Backpack — three-proof product demo

**Mode:** Image-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 12s · **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 · **Audio:** foley + voiceover

```text
Create a 12-second vertical creator demonstration for the exact charcoal commuter backpack in the reference image. Lock the bag's size, fabric, zipper count, seam pattern, top handle, side pocket, and color.

0–3s — overhead shot on a wooden table: hands pack a 14-inch laptop, headphones, notebook, and water bottle into separate visible compartments; each object appears once. 3–6s — match cut to the creator wearing the closed bag and walking through light rain; water beads on the fabric rather than soaking through. 6–9s — close-up of a hand smoothly opening the side-access zipper while the bag stays on one shoulder and removing only the headphones. 9–12s — bag placed upright beside a café chair; camera holds on the clean silhouette.

VOICEOVER: "Workday packed. Rain handled. Headphones still where I left them."

CAMERA: Realistic smartphone product demo, clean overhead cut, chest-level walking shot, macro zipper insert. Keep edits on hand motion.

PHYSICS: Objects fit without clipping; bag compresses slightly under weight; straps stay connected; raindrops follow gravity.

AVOID: floating objects, duplicated gear, changing pockets, logos, readable laptop branding, on-screen text, waterproof claims beyond the visible demo, watermark.
```

## 3. Night Serum — premium but believable skincare ad

**Mode:** Reference-to-video · **Duration:** 8s · **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 · **Audio:** ASMR foley

```text
Create an 8-second square skincare product film using the supplied unbranded blue glass serum bottle. Preserve the bottle, cap, pipette, label-free surface, liquid color, and exact proportions.

0–2s — bottle stands on a damp dark-stone pedestal; a narrow warm light moves slowly across the blue glass while condensation gathers but does not multiply unnaturally. 2–5s — a hand twists the cap once and lifts the pipette; macro focus follows one amber serum drop forming at the tip, stretching under gravity, then falling. 5–8s — the drop lands on a clean glass plate and spreads into a small luminous circle; rack focus returns to the upright bottle for a one-second hero hold.

CAMERA: Controlled macro product cinematography, 100mm lens feel, slow slider movement, precise focus pull.

PHYSICS: Liquid is viscous, one drop only, accurate refraction, cap threads align, bottle never changes size.

AUDIO: Cap twist, glass touch, single soft drop, subtle room tone. No dialogue and no music.

AVOID: skin transformation claims, medical symbols, extra bottles, label text, brand marks, smoke, excessive sparkles, warped glass, watermark.
```

## 4. Cordless Reading Lamp — problem/solution ad

**Mode:** Text-to-video · **Duration:** 10s · **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 · **Audio:** room ambience + dialogue

```text
Create a 10-second vertical UGC problem/solution ad for a fictional, unbranded cordless reading lamp with a matte terracotta dome, slim brass stem, circular base, and one touch button. Keep the lamp design identical in every shot.

0–3s — evening bedroom, creator tries to read while a harsh ceiling light fills the room; they squint and say, "This light is doing too much." 3–6s — match cut on their hand placing the cordless lamp on the bedside table and tapping the button once; the ceiling light turns off only after the lamp turns on. 6–9s — warm focused pool of light covers the book without spilling across the room; creator relaxes, adjusts the lamp angle at its real hinge, and keeps reading. 9–10s — simple product close-up with hand leaving frame.

CAMERA: Believable handheld phone video, two clean cuts, stable exposure transition, natural skin texture.

PHYSICS: Lamp remains grounded; hinge rotates on one axis; shadows and illumination respond to the lamp direction.

AUDIO: Room tone, page turn, touch click, exact spoken line with natural lip sync. No music.

AVOID: power cord appearing, impossible floating lamp, design drift, lighting before button press, captions, logos, watermark.
```

## 5. Smart Pet Feeder — trust-building product story

**Mode:** Image-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 12s · **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Audio:** pet + device foley

```text
Animate the reference photo into a 12-second home product story. Preserve the exact unbranded feeder, bowl, lid, camera opening, button placement, kibble color, kitchen floor, and the same adult tabby cat.

0–3s — low cat-eye angle; the cat waits beside the feeder and glances toward the kitchen doorway. 3–6s — the feeder emits a gentle two-note chime, its internal wheel turns, and one measured portion of kibble falls cleanly into the bowl. 6–9s — the cat approaches, sniffs once, then begins eating; the feeder remains stationary and the food level changes logically. 9–12s — cut to a close product-and-cat composition as an off-screen owner says, "Right on time." The cat briefly lifts its ears but continues eating.

CAMERA: Ground-level 35mm feel, one motivated cut, warm morning documentary realism.

PHYSICS: Accurate kibble collision and bounce; no food passes through the bowl; cat anatomy, markings, and scale stay consistent.

AUDIO: Quiet kitchen, soft paw steps, device motor, kibble impacts, gentle chime, one off-screen line. No music.

AVOID: cat talking, feeder redesign, excessive food, duplicated bowls, brand UI, phone screen, text, watermark.
```
