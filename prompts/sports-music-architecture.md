# Sports, Music, and Architecture Prompts for Grok Imagine 1.5

## 1. Final Bend — grounded sprint commercial

**Mode:** Image-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 10s · **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Audio:** stadium + breath

```text
Animate the reference image of a fictional adult sprinter on an empty rain-darkened track. Preserve face, teal racing kit with no logos, lane four position, shoe design, body proportions, stadium architecture, and overcast light.

0–3s — low side-tracking shot matches the athlete entering the final bend at controlled race speed; spikes contact the track and release tiny water sprays. 3–7s — camera moves to a front three-quarter tracking angle; effort increases visibly through arm drive, breathing, and focused expression without anatomy distortion. 7–10s — athlete crosses a plain white finish line, takes three realistic deceleration steps, then looks toward the empty stands as sunlight breaks through one cloud.

CAMERA: Sports-commercial realism, 70mm lens feel, smooth vehicle-level track, one cut motivated by passing a foreground marker, no speed ramp.

PHYSICS: Correct sprint foot contact, stable lane direction, realistic muscle and fabric motion, gradual deceleration, consistent water spray.

AUDIO: Spikes, breath, distant stadium ambience, light rain, no crowd roar until a subtle swell after the finish. No music or voiceover.

AVOID: superhuman speed, sliding feet, extra runners, body morphing, logos, timing board text, watermark.
```

## 2. Chalk and Resolve — climbing micro documentary

**Mode:** Text-to-video · **Duration:** 12s · **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 · **Audio:** natural foley + dialogue

```text
Create a 12-second vertical indoor bouldering documentary. A fictional adult climber wears a forest-green T-shirt, gray trousers, blue climbing shoes, and a chalk bag. A thick safety mat fully covers the floor; no rope is needed for this low wall.

0–4s — close-up: the climber rubs chalk into both hands, looks up at three orange holds, and quietly says, "One clean move." 4–9s — side view captures one controlled sequence: right hand to the first hold, left foot presses, hips shift toward the wall, left hand reaches the top hold. Every contact is visible. 9–12s — climber stabilizes, taps the top hold with both hands, then drops safely onto the mat with bent knees and smiles.

CAMERA: Intimate handheld documentary, 35mm, one cut from hands to full-body side view, keep all limbs visible during the move.

PHYSICS: Body weight transfers through real contact points, no floating, chalk dust is subtle, landing compresses the mat.

AUDIO: Gym room tone, chalk rub, shoe friction, breathing, exact short line, soft landing. No music.

AVOID: dangerous height, missing mat, impossible reach, extra fingers, changing hold layout, brand marks, subtitles, watermark.
```

## 3. One-Take Midnight Jazz Session

**Mode:** Reference-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 15s · **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Audio:** synchronized performance

```text
Create a 15-second one-take music film from the supplied fictional trio reference. Preserve the same adult upright-bass player, brushed-drum player, electric-piano player, instruments, wardrobe, studio layout, amber practical lamps, and midnight blue window.

0–5s — camera begins close on the bassist's right hand plucking a simple original three-note walking pattern; strings visibly vibrate in sync. 5–10s — slow arc reveals the drummer brushing the snare in time while the pianist adds two restrained chords; musicians exchange brief eye contact. 10–15s — camera settles into a balanced trio frame as all land on one soft final chord; the bass resonance decays and everyone holds still for the room sound.

CAMERA: One continuous slow 60-degree arc, 50mm cinema feel, shallow depth of field but each musician comes into focus as featured.

AUDIO: Generate an original, minimal late-night jazz phrase only—upright bass, brushed snare, warm electric piano. Tight visible synchronization, natural studio reflections, no vocals, no recognizable melody.

PHYSICS: Correct hand-to-instrument contact, believable string and drum response, no instrument geometry changes.

AVOID: extra musicians, impossible fingering, audience, labels, sheet-music text, cuts, watermark.
```

## 4. Courtyard House at First Light — real estate reveal

**Mode:** Image-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 12s · **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Audio:** environmental

```text
Animate the supplied architectural reference into a 12-second premium real-estate reveal. Preserve the exact fictional single-story courtyard house, window count, timber screens, pale stone, furniture placement, shallow reflecting pool, plants, and surrounding landscape.

0–4s — camera glides slowly through the open entry toward the courtyard while pre-dawn blue light fills the interior; all doors remain fixed. 4–8s — sunrise reaches the far wall, warm light travels across the pale stone and reflects softly into the ceiling; a small breeze moves only the courtyard grasses and sheer curtain. 8–12s — camera stops at a wide symmetrical view as one fictional resident places a cup on the outdoor table and leaves frame, demonstrating scale without blocking the architecture.

CAMERA: Smooth stabilized gimbal, 24mm architectural lens, straight vertical lines, constant slow speed, no cuts.

PHYSICS: Reflections match geometry, curtain and grasses share breeze direction, pool water has subtle ripples, sunlight advances plausibly.

AUDIO: Morning birds, soft footsteps, cup on wood, light water movement. No voiceover or music.

AVOID: changing floor plan, bending walls, opening new doors, floating furniture, exaggerated lens distortion, sale text, logos, watermark.
```

## 5. Studio Corner Transformation — interior design before/after

**Mode:** Video editing · **Duration:** source duration, maximum 8.7s · **Aspect ratio:** match source · **Audio:** room foley

```text
Edit only the interior finishes and furniture in the supplied locked-off studio-corner video. Keep the room dimensions, two windows, door, floor level, camera position, daylight direction, and all wall openings unchanged.

TRANSFORMATION: Over the first half, replace the existing bulky dark furniture with a compact oak desk, one ergonomic cream chair, a low modular shelf, one floor lamp, and three small plants. Change the wall paint to warm off-white and add a muted blue woven rug. Over the second half, let the transformed room settle into a clean final view with gentle curtain movement.

TRANSITION STYLE: Objects should assemble through believable sliding and placement motion along the floor, not dissolve, explode, or grow from nothing. One object moves at a time; furniture never intersects walls or each other.

LIGHTING: Preserve the original natural-light direction and exposure; update contact shadows and reflections to match each new object.

AUDIO: Soft wood placement, fabric movement, one lamp switch click near the end, original room tone. No voice or music.

INVARIANTS: Do not change architecture, window view, camera, ceiling height, floor material, or source duration.

AVOID: impossible floor-plan changes, extra windows, floating decor, readable art text, brand marks, people, watermark.
```
