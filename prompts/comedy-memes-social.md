## 1. Quarterly Pigeon Review — office animal comedy

**Mode:** Image-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 9s · **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Audio:** dialogue + foley

```text
Animate the supplied original 3D office-comedy image for 9 seconds. Preserve the orange cat presenter, navy tie, blank circular chart, pointer, eight pigeon coworkers, conference table, robot vacuum, and open door. No new characters.

0–3s — the cat taps the totally blank chart twice with the pointer and says in a serious voice, "As you can see, the circle is up." The pigeons exchange confused side glances. 3–6s — the robot vacuum quietly rolls through the doorway carrying one loose feather; only the pigeon nearest the door notices and slowly turns its head. 6–9s — one pigeon raises a wing to ask a question; the cat immediately flips the chart to reveal another blank circle and says, "Questions after snacks." All pigeons nod at once.

CAMERA: Locked wide ensemble framing with one subtle push on the final line.

MOTION: Stylized but grounded 3D animation, clear anticipation and reaction poses, feathers and tie have slight secondary motion.

AUDIO: Pointer taps, room ventilation, vacuum hum fading down hallway, two exact lines with clean lip sync, small synchronized pigeon coo at the end.

AVOID: text on chart, extra pigeons, human coworkers, slapstick violence, famous cartoon style, logos, captions, watermark.
```

## 2. Weather Dog vs. Leaf — seamless vertical loop

**Mode:** Text-to-video · **Duration:** 6s · **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 · **Audio:** wind + reaction

```text
Create a seamless 6-second vertical loop styled as a cheerful local weather report. A fictional fluffy gray dog sits behind a tiny outdoor desk wearing a yellow rain hat; a blank blue board stands behind with no text.

ACTION: The dog looks confidently into camera as if presenting. A strong but safe gust blows one red maple leaf from right to left. The leaf sticks over the dog's nose; the dog freezes, eyes cross toward the leaf, then gives one short sneeze. The sneeze blows the leaf back to the exact starting side of frame while the dog returns to the same confident pose, creating a clean loop.

CAMERA: Locked medium vertical composition, no zoom or cut.

PHYSICS: Hat stays on, fur and leaf respond to one consistent wind direction until the sneeze reverses only the leaf, desk remains grounded.

AUDIO: Light wind, distant birds, leaf rustle, one small dog sneeze. No speech, music, or laugh track.

AVOID: distressed animal, human mouth, extra leaves, moving background, text, logo, subtitles, watermark.
```

## 3. Elevator to the Wrong Century — deadpan live action

**Mode:** Text-to-video · **Duration:** 10s · **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 · **Audio:** dialogue + ambience

```text
Create a 10-second vertical deadpan comedy in one fixed modern office elevator. A fictional adult office worker in a beige suit holds one reusable lunch container. Keep identity, clothes, lunch, elevator walls, and camera fixed.

0–3s — elevator doors open to reveal a windswept prehistoric-looking fern valley with one distant harmless long-necked creature; the worker looks out, checks the blank elevator button panel, and does not exit. 3–6s — doors close, soft chime, then open again to an ornate but fictional candlelit ballroom where masked guests pause and stare. The worker stays inside. 6–10s — doors close and reopen to the normal office. A coworker waits outside. The worker calmly says, "Take the stairs today," and walks out.

CAMERA: Locked phone-camera framing from the back elevator corner; transformations happen only beyond the doors.

PHYSICS: Doors follow the same track and timing, light spills into the elevator consistently, lunch container never changes hands.

AUDIO: Elevator hum and chime, brief valley wind, brief ballroom murmur, exact final dialogue. No music.

AVOID: famous historical figures, monster attack, changing elevator interior, extra limbs, button labels, subtitles, logos, watermark.
```

## 4. The Great Strawberry Heist — tiny mascot caper

**Mode:** Text-to-video · **Duration:** 8s · **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 · **Audio:** playful foley

```text
Create an 8-second square miniature caper with three original round felt mascots—mint green, lavender, and orange—trying to move one real strawberry across a kitchen counter using a thread pulley.

0–3s — wide macro view: green and lavender pull the thread while orange guides the strawberry on a bottle-cap cart. The heavy fruit rolls slowly. 3–6s — one strawberry leaf catches on a wooden spoon; tension builds, the thread stretches, then the leaf pops free and all three mascots tumble backward into a soft pile. 6–8s — the cart coasts perfectly into a tiny paper garage; one strawberry seed falls off and the mascots celebrate it as if it were treasure.

STYLE: Handcrafted felt stop motion, real kitchen textures, visible stitches, original character designs.

PHYSICS: Strawberry has convincing mass, thread stays connected, cart wheels rotate, collision is gentle and readable.

AUDIO: Tiny effort squeaks without words, thread creak, spoon twang, soft felt impacts, miniature celebratory bell.

AVOID: known mascots, food waste, human hands, fruit changing size, text, brand packaging, watermark.
```

## 5. Robot Vacuum Escape Cam — faux security footage

**Mode:** Text-to-video · **Duration:** 7s · **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Audio:** room foley

```text
Create a 7-second fictional home-security-camera comedy clip with a wide, slightly elevated static view of a bright living room. A black unbranded robot vacuum wears a small paper party hat; a sleepy adult basset hound lies safely on a rug.

0–2s — the vacuum approaches a lightweight curtain that lifts in an open patio breeze; the dog watches without moving. 2–5s — as the curtain rises, the vacuum crosses the open doorway onto a covered patio, its party hat wobbling; the dog slowly sits up, surprised. 5–7s — the vacuum makes a tidy U-turn and comes back inside carrying one fallen flower petal on top; the dog lies down again exactly as the curtain returns to its opening position for a loopable finish.

CAMERA: Static security-camera perspective, normal lens, natural daylight, no timestamp overlay.

PHYSICS: Vacuum stays on flat surfaces, curtain follows breeze, hat remains attached, dog anatomy and markings remain consistent.

AUDIO: Vacuum motor, curtain flutter, one collar jingle, patio birds. No voice or music.

AVOID: stairs, street escape, danger, distressed animal, timestamp, surveillance brand, captions, watermark.
```
