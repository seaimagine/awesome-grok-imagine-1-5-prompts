## 1. Wind Couriers — original rooftop chase

**Mode:** Text-to-video · **Duration:** 10s · **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Audio:** wind + impacts

```text
Create a 10-second original hand-drawn 2D action sequence. Two adult sky couriers race across tiled floating rooftops during a violet storm. Courier A wears a mustard jacket with a long indigo scarf; Courier B wears a slate-blue jacket and carries a cylindrical map case. Preserve designs, colors, faces, and left-to-right travel direction.

0–3s — low side-tracking shot as both sprint across wet tiles; footfalls displace small water splashes and the scarf trails with delayed motion. 3–7s — the roof ends; Courier B vaults the gap while Courier A sweeps one arm, forming a translucent ribbon of compressed wind that briefly supports their next step. 7–10s — both land on the next roof with believable weight; tiles crack under the harder landing, they exchange one competitive grin, and continue out of frame.

STYLE: Premium original 2D animation, expressive key poses, detailed backgrounds, selective motion smear, clean linework, no resemblance to an existing franchise.

CAMERA: Continuous lateral tracking with one low-angle arc during the jump; readable silhouettes.

AUDIO: Storm wind, shoe impacts, tile cracks, cloth snaps, a rising airy whoosh. No dialogue or music.

AVOID: costume changes, extra weapons, duplicated limbs, weightless jumps, random lightning strikes, copyrighted characters, text, logos, watermark.
```

## 2. Watercolor Train Memory — poetic transformation

**Mode:** Image-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 9s · **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Audio:** train ambience

```text
Animate the supplied watercolor illustration while preserving the same fictional adult passenger, cream coat, red suitcase, train carriage, seat layout, and handmade paper texture.

0–3s — passenger watches a green valley through the window; only their breathing, one blink, and subtle train vibration move. 3–6s — sunlight passes across the window and the outside landscape gently loosens into flowing watercolor pigment, with blue and green washes moving along the paper fibers while the carriage stays solid. 6–9s — the color wash gathers into the shape of a distant seaside town, then settles back into a stable painted view; the passenger rests one hand on the suitcase and smiles faintly.

CAMERA: Locked three-quarter composition with a very slow push; no cuts.

MATERIAL BEHAVIOR: Pigment blooms and capillary edges behave like real wet watercolor on textured paper. The passenger and interior retain clean outlines and do not melt.

AUDIO: Rail rhythm, quiet carriage creak, distant soft whistle, paper-brush texture during the transformation. No dialogue or score.

AVOID: face drift, carriage geometry changes, full-frame liquid distortion, photorealism, text, station signs, logos, watermark.
```

## 3. Tiny Baker, Giant Croissant — stop-motion rescue

**Mode:** Text-to-video · **Duration:** 11s · **Aspect ratio:** 4:3 · **Audio:** playful foley

```text
Create an 11-second original stop-motion comedy-action scene on a miniature bakery set. A thumb-sized felt baker with round glasses, blue overalls, and a white paper hat tries to stop a freshly baked croissant as large as a cart from rolling off a wooden cooling table.

0–3s — the giant croissant begins rolling after the table tilts; the baker looks up, drops a tiny brush, and runs after it. 3–7s — side view: the baker throws a loop of red kitchen twine around the croissant; the line tightens and pulls the baker across scattered flour, leaving two small tracks. 7–11s — the baker braces both feet against a butter dish, the twine stretches, and the croissant stops at the table edge. A single sesame seed falls onto the baker's hat; they look upward in exhausted disbelief.

STYLE: Handcrafted felt, paper, wood, real flour, visible stitch details, charming 12-fps stop-motion cadence.

PHYSICS: Croissant rolls around its curved body, twine stays taut, flour reacts to sliding feet, objects retain miniature scale.

AUDIO: Wooden rumble, quick felt footsteps, twine tension creak, soft stop, tiny seed tap. Light pizzicato rhythm without known melody.

AVOID: famous studio style, plastic CGI finish, size changes, human hands, speech, text, watermark.
```

## 4. Ink Circle Duel — graphic martial-arts abstraction

**Mode:** Text-to-video · **Duration:** 8s · **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 · **Audio:** brush + percussion

```text
Create an 8-second square graphic-animation duel on warm rice paper. Two original adult martial artists are represented with economical black ink shapes: one has a square shoulder silhouette and red waist cord; the other has a long coat silhouette and blue wrist wraps.

0–2s — one wet black brush circle paints itself clockwise; both figures step into the empty center from opposite sides. 2–5s — three precisely readable exchanges: palm deflection, low sweep avoided by a hop, then both stop an inch apart with opposing forearms locked. Each action leaves one short dry-brush trail that fades, not a cloud of effects. 5–8s — they release the lock and bow; the black circle completes with one final ink splash, while the red cord and blue wraps remain the only color.

CAMERA: Top-down orthographic view, no zoom or cuts. Strong negative space and clean silhouettes.

MATERIAL: Realistic ink absorption, feathered wet edges, granular dry strokes, paper remains flat.

AUDIO: Brush on paper, three hand/cloth impacts, one low drum hit on the lock, quiet room breath on the bow.

AVOID: weapons, blood, extra fighters, unreadable motion, calligraphy characters, logos, text, watermark.
```

## 5. Harbor Mecha Rescue — weighty retro animation

**Mode:** Text-to-video · **Duration:** 15s · **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Audio:** machinery + radio

```text
Create a 15-second original retro-futurist 2D animated rescue at a storm-damaged harbor. A compact orange maintenance mecha with four broad feet, two utility arms, and a circular blue cockpit visor lifts a collapsed crane beam away from a small ferry. Keep every mechanical part, color, and scale consistent.

0–4s — wide harbor view in heavy wind; the mecha plants all four feet on wet concrete and clamps both arms around the steel beam. 4–9s — low close angle as hydraulic joints compress, feet settle under load, rain runs off panels, and the beam slowly rises with visible weight; no effortless motion. 9–13s — the ferry reverses through the opening while crew wave from a safe deck position. 13–15s — the mecha lowers the beam onto clear ground, vents one burst of steam, and the pilot says over radio, "Channel is clear."

STYLE: Original hand-painted cel animation with modern compositing, restrained color palette, no resemblance to existing robot franchises.

AUDIO: Rain, harbor alarm, metal strain, hydraulic pumps, ferry engine, radio-filtered line. No heroic music.

AVOID: combat, weapons, explosions, changing robot geometry, floating beam, human injuries, text, insignia, watermark.
```
