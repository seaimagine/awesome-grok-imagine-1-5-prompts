# Creative Editing and Transition Prompts for Grok Imagine 1.5

![Original match-cut and transition preview](../assets/creative-editing.png)

These prompts focus on a single visual anchor, motivated transitions, video edits, and extensions. They are written for Grok Imagine 1.5's short-form timing: fewer transitions, stronger continuity, and a clear end state.

## 1. Origami Crane Across Four Worlds — beat-synced match cuts

**Mode:** Text-to-video · **Duration:** 12s · **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Audio:** original beat + environmental accents

```text
Create a 12-second cinematic match-cut film around one palm-sized white origami crane. The crane remains precisely centered, identical in fold geometry, scale, orientation, and sharp focus throughout. Only the support surface, background, light, and surrounding atmosphere change.

TIMELINE:
0–3s — rainy night train platform; the crane stands on wet stone, cool rain and a passing train reflected behind it. On the first bass hit, a round raindrop lands beside the crane and fills the frame with a circular ripple.
3–6s — match cut from the ripple to a bright sun ring over a dry salt flat; the crane stands on cracked pale ground, its paper edges lifting slightly in a warm crosswind. On the next beat, wind-blown dust crosses the lens from left to right.
6–9s — the dust becomes stage haze in a small original live-music venue; red and amber lights sweep behind the crane while a speaker vibration makes it tremble without changing position. A cymbal flash briefly fills frame.
9–12s — the flash becomes soft dawn through a moss forest; the crane rests on a dark stone as one real feather drifts down beside it. Hold the final quiet frame for one second.

CAMERA: Same low 50mm-equivalent composition in all four scenes; cuts occur only on full-frame occlusion, ripple, dust, or light. No zoom, orbit, or reframing.

PHYSICS: Paper reacts to rain, dry wind, vibration, and forest humidity without unfolding. Shadows and reflections match each environment.

AUDIO: One original four-beat electronic pulse binds the transitions. Layer train rumble and rain, dry wind, venue crowd and cymbal, then forest birds. Environmental sound changes exactly on each cut.

AVOID: text on the paper, changing crane shape, duplicated crane, split-screen borders, random fifth scene, copyrighted music, logos, subtitles, watermark.
```

## 2. Doorway Through the Seasons — spatially locked transition

**Mode:** Image-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 10s · **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 · **Audio:** seasonal ambience

```text
Animate the supplied vertical image of a fictional adult standing inside a simple apartment doorway. Preserve the exact person, cream sweater, dark trousers, doorway geometry, wood floor, camera position, and interior lighting. The person remains indoors and never crosses the threshold.

0–2.5s — beyond the open door is a spring courtyard with light rain and fresh green leaves; the person extends one palm and catches a drop.
2.5–5s — a foreground umbrella carried past the lens creates a full-frame wipe; outside becomes a bright summer courtyard. The person's hand is now dry and lowers naturally; warm air moves the sweater cuff.
5–7.5s — a large amber leaf blows toward the lens and briefly covers it; outside becomes autumn with a few dry leaves skittering along the same path.
7.5–10s — the person gently closes the door halfway; during the door-edge occlusion, the outside changes to quiet winter snow. They reopen it to the original angle, smile softly, and hold.

CAMERA: Locked vertical camera, fixed lens and exposure reference. Every transition is motivated by a real foreground occlusion.

CONTINUITY: Interior, subject identity, clothes, body position, door angle at each reveal, and screen direction stay consistent. Only the exterior season and its corresponding light may change.

AUDIO: Spring rain, summer insects, autumn leaves, winter hush and one distant bell. Smooth audio crossfades centered on each visual wipe. No dialogue or music.

AVOID: rapid plant growth, interior season changes, costume changes, door redesign, snow indoors, morphing face, text, logo, watermark.
```

## 3. Clean the Plaza — remove background people from a source video

**Mode:** Video editing · **Duration:** match source, maximum 8.7s · **Aspect ratio:** match source · **Audio:** preserve source ambience

```text
Edit the supplied locked-off plaza video. Remove every background pedestrian and cyclist while preserving the single lead performer in the mustard coat exactly as recorded.

CHANGE ONLY: Reconstruct the paving stones, fountain edge, benches, trees, and distant storefront surfaces hidden by removed people. Keep natural fountain water, moving leaves, cloud light, reflections, and the lead performer's original motion.

PRESERVE EXACTLY: Source duration, frame rate, aspect ratio, camera position, lens, crop, lead identity, mustard coat, hair, walking path, body timing, shadow, fountain geometry, bench placement, weather, and source audio.

TEMPORAL CONSISTENCY: Reconstructed background textures remain stable frame to frame. No ghost silhouettes, smeared limbs, duplicated shadows, flicker, or sudden paving changes where people were removed.

AUDIO: Keep the original plaza ambience. Remove only clearly isolated bicycle bell or nearby speech tied to deleted subjects when possible; do not replace the ambience with silence or music.

AVOID: beautifying the lead, changing walking speed, stabilizing or reframing the source, adding plants or furniture, empty-looking frozen water, text, logo, watermark.
```

## 4. Continue the Kite Flight — controlled video extension

**Mode:** Video extension · **Duration:** 6–8s continuation · **Aspect ratio:** match source · **Audio:** continue source soundscape

```text
Extend the supplied video from its exact final frame. The source ends with a fictional adult on a grassy hill holding a taut red kite line while the red diamond kite climbs toward the upper-right corner at golden hour.

CONTINUATION: Maintain the same person, clothing, hill, kite, line attachment, wind direction, sun position, lens, camera height, color grade, and rightward screen direction. The camera continues the existing slow pan right at the same speed.

0–3s of extension — a steady gust pulls the kite higher; the person takes one grounded step forward to maintain balance, bends the elbow, and lets out a short controlled length of line. Grass, loose clothing, and kite tail all move toward the left under the same wind.
3–6s — the kite crosses in front of a thin sunlit cloud, briefly becoming a clean silhouette. The person laughs once off camera; the line stays visible and physically connected.
6–8s optional — camera eases to a stop as the kite stabilizes near the upper center. Hold the kite and warm sky for a clean edit point.

AUDIO: Continue the original wind and distant birds without a seam; add reel clicks, one natural laugh, and fabric flutter. No new music.

AVOID: resetting to an earlier frame, teleporting the kite, severed line, reversed wind, new characters, weather change, camera jump, text, watermark.
```

## 5. Mug-to-Moon Transition — shape match with two references

**Mode:** Reference-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 9s · **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 · **Audio:** café-to-night transition

```text
Use Reference Image 1 only for the exact unbranded blue ceramic mug and warm café table. Use Reference Image 2 only for the fictional observatory terrace, telescope silhouette, and full moon placement. Do not copy people or written elements from either reference.

Create a 9-second square shape-match transition.

0–3s — macro top-down café view. A hand gently stirs dark coffee once; the circular liquid surface and pale foam ring remain centered. Camera begins a slow vertical push toward the cup.
3–5s — the coffee reflection darkens into a star field while the pale foam ring becomes the edge of a full moon. The transition occurs inside the circular cup only; mug rim stays visible until the moon exactly fills it.
5–9s — pull back from the matching full moon to reveal the observatory terrace at night. A fictional adult astronomer adjusts the telescope by one small movement, then steps back to look up. Hold the moon centered above the telescope.

CAMERA: One smooth push followed by a matched pullback on the same optical axis. No spin or sideways jump.

PHYSICS: Stirring creates a decaying spiral; liquid volume stays constant; the observatory is stable and grounded.

AUDIO: Spoon on ceramic and café room tone gradually filter into night wind, one telescope adjustment click, and distant insects. No speech or music.

AVOID: visible brand, extra mug, liquid splash, moon changing size after reveal, magical particles outside the cup, readable signage, watermark.
```
