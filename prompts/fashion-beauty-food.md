## 1. Silver Tides — luxury fashion motion film

**Mode:** Image-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 10s · **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Audio:** fabric + water

```text
Animate the supplied fashion reference into a 10-second original editorial film. Preserve the fictional adult model's exact face, high-neck silver cape, tailored trousers, shoes, hairstyle, full coverage, pool depth, moon backlight, and monochrome palette.

0–3s — low centered view as the model takes one deliberate step through the shallow black pool; a small realistic wave spreads from the shoe. 3–7s — camera tracks backward at the same speed; the cape's long metallic fabric strips lag behind the movement, rise on a controlled side breeze, and cross without fusing. 7–10s — the model stops, the heavy fabric continues half a beat then settles, and the water reflection reforms; hold the final symmetrical pose for one second.

CAMERA: 50mm editorial cinema look, low dolly backward, no orbit, crisp face focus and deep enough focus for the garment silhouette.

PHYSICS: Water displacement follows each footstep; fabric has weight, tension, and gravity; reflections match the moving garment.

AUDIO: Shallow water, metallic textile whisper, slow footsteps, large quiet studio ambience. No dialogue or music.

AVOID: exposed body, sensual posing, face drift, extra fabric limbs, liquid metal touching skin, floating feet, logos, text, watermark.
```

## 2. Mineral Mist — unbranded fragrance visual

**Mode:** Reference-to-video · **Duration:** 8s · **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 · **Audio:** macro foley

```text
Create an 8-second square product film from the supplied unbranded frosted-glass fragrance bottle. Preserve the rectangular bottle, stone-gray cap, clear liquid level, blank surface, proportions, and centered placement.

0–2s — bottle sits on a pale mineral slab in cool dawn light; camera slides six inches left while fine mist drifts behind, never hiding the silhouette. 2–5s — cap lifts straight up by an unseen mechanism and stops above the bottle; one clean spray releases diagonally into the backlight, revealing micro-droplets. 5–8s — cap returns and seats precisely; a warm sunrise reflection crosses the glass, then the camera holds on the bottle.

CAMERA: 100mm macro product lens feel, controlled slider, no dramatic spin.

PHYSICS: Droplets disperse and fall naturally, cap follows one vertical path, glass refraction and liquid level stay consistent.

AUDIO: Soft cap release, single sprayer click, delicate mist, stone room ambience. No voice or music.

AVOID: brand marks, label invention, repeated spraying, bottle deformation, smoke clouds, flowers appearing, text, watermark.
```

## 3. Orbit Ring — jewelry macro reveal

**Mode:** Text-to-video · **Duration:** 7s · **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 · **Audio:** minimalist foley

```text
Create a 7-second vertical macro jewelry film for a fictional sculptural ring: brushed recycled silver, one small oval green stone, and an asymmetric open band. Keep the exact design consistent.

0–2s — extreme macro of the ring resting on dark folded paper; a narrow light reveals the brushed grain from left to right. 2–5s — the paper slowly unfolds through one hinge-like crease, causing the ring to roll exactly half a turn and stop with the green stone facing camera. 5–7s — focus moves from the band texture to the stone's internal inclusions as a tiny highlight travels once across its surface.

CAMERA: 120mm macro feel, locked camera, very shallow focus with deliberate rack, high-end catalog realism.

PHYSICS: Ring rolls according to its asymmetric weight; paper bends along existing creases; metal and stone reflections remain coherent.

AUDIO: Paper fiber creak, soft metal contact, one subtle glassy tone at the final highlight.

AVOID: fingers, duplicated jewelry, gemstone growth, perfect mirror chrome, text, logo, price, watermark.
```

## 4. Citrus Caramel Crack — restaurant ASMR

**Mode:** Image-to-video recommended · **Duration:** 8s · **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Audio:** ASMR only

![Original food ASMR preview](../assets/food-asmr.png)

```text
Animate the supplied dessert reference into an 8-second macro ASMR clip. Preserve the chef's hands, citrus dessert shape, caramel shell color, ceramic plate, table position, and warm side lighting.

0–2s — the chef's fingertips grip opposite edges of the thin caramel shell above the dessert; steam rises gently from the citrus center. 2–5s — hands bend the shell until it produces one crisp crack; three to six small shards fall onto the plate while the two larger pieces remain held. 5–8s — camera pushes closer as one shard lands against the dessert and a drop of citrus glaze slowly runs down the side; hands move out of frame and the plated result holds.

CAMERA: 100mm food macro, slow six-inch push, precise focus on crack point then glaze.

PHYSICS: Brittle caramel fractures sharply, shards fall under gravity and bounce once, glaze remains viscous, hands stay anatomically correct.

AUDIO: Quiet restaurant pass, single detailed caramel snap, delicate ceramic taps, faint steam hiss. No voice or music.

AVOID: exploding shell, excessive fragments, changing dessert, extra fingers, utensils appearing, text, logo, watermark.
```

## 5. Garden to Plate — farm-to-table micro story

**Mode:** Text-to-video · **Duration:** 15s · **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 · **Audio:** natural foley

```text
Create a 15-second farm-to-table micro story with one fictional adult cook in a navy linen shirt and tan apron. Preserve face, tied-back hair, clothing, and the same striped heirloom tomato through the sequence.

0–4s — garden macro: the cook twists one dew-covered striped tomato from the vine; morning backlight reveals fine surface texture. 4–8s — match cut on the tomato into a bright kitchen; one chef's knife slices it cleanly, showing realistic seeds and juice. 8–12s — the cook places three slices on toasted bread with white cheese and basil, using deliberate hands. 12–15s — medium shot outdoors: the finished toast is set on a wooden table; the cook sits, exhales, and the camera settles on steam from a nearby cup.

CAMERA: Natural commercial documentary, macro-to-medium progression, cuts motivated by hand motion, no speed ramp.

PHYSICS: Tomato remains the same variety and size, knife contact is accurate, food does not duplicate, dew and juice behave naturally.

AUDIO: Garden birds, stem snap, knife on board, toast crunch, leaves in breeze. No dialogue, narration, or music.

AVOID: extra ingredients, unsafe knife work, changing kitchen, beauty retouching, branded packaging, subtitles, watermark.
```
